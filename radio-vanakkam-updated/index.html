Our Story

About Us

Radio Vanakkam was born from a deep love for Tamil culture and a passion to connect Tamil speakers worldwide through the universal language of music.

Since our inception, we have been committed to preserving the rich heritage of Tamil music while embracing contemporary sounds. From the soul-stirring compositions of classical maestros to the chart-topping hits of today, we bring you a diverse audio experience that celebrates Tamil artistry in all its forms.

Our team of passionate RJs and music curators work tirelessly to deliver content that entertains, educates, and connects our global Tamil community. Whether you're in Chennai, Toronto, Singapore, or anywhere else in the world – Radio Vanakkam is your companion, your connection to home.
